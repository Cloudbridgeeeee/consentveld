How a ConsentVeld setup runs

  1. Upload your privacy documents

    Send your notices, processor agreements and policies, and connect preference exports.

  2. Read the privacy register

    Within a week you see every document, its type, owner and review date.

  3. Review the policy comparisons

    Your privacy lead confirms or rejects each gap and the register records the decision.

Questions

What compliance teams ask before a demo.

Ask about a demo

Who is ConsentVeld for?

Adult wellness retailers, marketplaces and platforms that handle sensitive customer data.

Does it give legal advice?

No. It flags possible gaps; your privacy lead or adviser decides.

What does it cost?

A subscription per organisation. We quote after the demo.

How is customer data protected?

Each organisation’s records are encrypted with its own KMS key and never shared.

Does it store purchase details?

No. It holds privacy documents and preference records, not order contents.
