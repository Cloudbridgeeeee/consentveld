# Product

ConsentVeld: Responsible privacy management for adult wellness.

AI privacy preference management software that helps adult wellness organizations classify privacy documentation and manage consent-related administrative records.

- Founder and CEO: Benjamin Nwankwo
- Co-founder and CTO: Aisha Mohammed
- Industry: SexTech, Data Privacy, RegTech
- Customers: Adult wellness marketplaces, retailers and specialized digital platforms
- Core features: Privacy document classification, preference organization, policy comparison, review reminders, audit reports
- MVP: Privacy preference dashboards and professionally reviewed policy comparisons
- Planned AWS: Bedrock, Textract, S3, KMS, DynamoDB
- Business model: Organization-level subscriptions
- Main action: Book a demo
